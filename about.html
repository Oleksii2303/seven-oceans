Our Story

Seven Oceans Emergency Food Ration is the result of over 50 years of Norwegian maritime engineering — trusted by sailors, fishermen, and rescue teams navigating the world's roughest seas.

Manufactured by COMPACT Food Solutions in Norway, every bar is engineered for survival: 5-year shelf life, waterproof packaging, and complete balanced nutrition. No refrigeration. No compromises.

For over 5 years, we've been bringing this proven Norwegian technology to customers across the United States — and we're proud to be the #1 Best Seller on Amazon in the Emergency Food category, with thousands of satisfied customers trusting us every year.

We work directly with the manufacturer to guarantee authenticity, freshness, and full certification on every shipment. No middlemen. No fakes. Just genuine Norwegian survival food.
